FAQ

Cruise & wine-tour questions

How early will you get me to the ship?

We time pickup to your cruise line's boarding window — comfortably ahead of the cut-off without hours of standing around. Tell us the line and sailing time and we'll set it.

What if my ship gets back late?

We track the ship's actual arrival and adjust the return pickup. You won't be waiting at the pier and you won't be charged for our wait.

How many wineries can we do in a day?

In Woodinville, four to six tasting rooms in an afternoon is a relaxed pace. Farther out, plan on two or three plus lunch.

How big can the group be?

Up to 3 in the sedan, 6 in the SUV, 14 in the Sprinter van. Larger groups can book more than one vehicle.

Is there a minimum for wine tours?

Three hours (four for the Sprinter). Most Woodinville days comfortably clear that.

Can you carry cases we buy?

Yes — there's room, and the driver will help load. Just leave space for the people.
