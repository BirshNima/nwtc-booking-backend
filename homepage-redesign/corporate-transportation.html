Corporate FAQ

What travel managers ask

How does billing work?

One itemized invoice at the end of each month, on net terms agreed at setup (commonly Net 15). Each line ties to a trip, a booker, and a cost code if you use them.

Can several people book on one account?

Yes. You name the authorized bookers; each can reserve and see the account's trips. Changes and cancellations go through the dedicated line.

What areas and vehicles are covered?

The full King and Pierce county service area, plus North Sound cities, in sedans, SUVs, and Sprinter vans. Regional airport transfers are quoted per trip.

What's your cancellation policy for accounts?

Set in your agreement. For most accounts, cancellations more than a few hours out are free; last-minute and same-day follow a standard fee schedule.

Are you licensed and insured for corporate work?

Yes — a registered WUTC carrier with commercial insurance. We'll provide a certificate of insurance and our operating authority on request.
