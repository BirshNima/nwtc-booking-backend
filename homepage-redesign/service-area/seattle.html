Seattle isn't one drive — it's dozens. From downtown, Belltown, and South Lake Union it's the SR‑99 tunnel or I‑5 south, 20–30 minutes off-peak. From West Seattle it's the bridge to 1st Ave S or SR‑509, often the fastest run in the city at 15–25 minutes. From North Seattle — Ballard, Green Lake, Northgate, the U‑District — add 10–20 minutes for the trip down I‑5 through the core, which is where Seattle traffic actually lives. Your driver picks the route for the hour, routes around the SoDo stadium gridlock on event nights, and accounts for the SR‑99 tunnel toll.
