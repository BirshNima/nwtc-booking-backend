Gig Harbor to SEA is SR‑16 east across the Tacoma Narrows Bridge, then I‑5 north through Tacoma. Two things shape the drive: the Narrows Bridge toll — charged eastbound, toward Tacoma, so you pay it on the way to the airport, and we build it into the flat rate — and the SR‑16 backup that forms at the bridge and again at the Nalley Valley interchange in Tacoma during rush and on summer weekends. For a morning flight, plan a Gig Harbor pickup about 3.75 hours before departure on a weekday.

What Gig Harbor rides usually are

Gig Harbor books airport travel and a strong flow of cruise transfers — the waterfront and retirement demographic sails from the Seattle piers and much prefers to be driven than deal with the bridge, the parking, and a week-long garage bill. St. Anthony Hospital books assisted transportation, and residents book hourly service for a Seattle day or a night at the theater without driving back across the Narrows after dark.

Gig Harbor FAQ

Is the Tacoma Narrows Bridge toll included in the rate?

Yes. The toll is charged eastbound — toward Tacoma, so you pay it heading to the airport — and it's built into the flat $139 sedan / $169 SUV rate. There's no toll on the return.

How long is the drive from Gig Harbor to Sea-Tac?

45–55 minutes off-peak via SR‑16 and I‑5. The SR‑16 backup at the bridge and the Tacoma Nalley Valley interchange can push it to 60–80 in rush or on a summer weekend.

Do you do cruise transfers from Gig Harbor?

Yes — Pier 66 and Pier 91, timed to your cruise line's boarding window, with luggage help. It saves a week of downtown Seattle parking. See cruise transfers.

Is the $139 rate the same from Fox Island and downtown Gig Harbor?

Yes — one flat Gig Harbor rate to SEA, either direction. Extra stops are $20 each.
