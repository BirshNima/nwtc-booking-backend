Edmonds to SEA is I‑5 south through Seattle or SR‑99 (Aurora) as the surface route — the same congested corridor Lynnwood deals with, plus the extra few miles. From the downtown Edmonds bowl the trick is getting out cleanly: SR‑104 past the ferry terminal can be blocked by holding lanes when a boat's loading, so we time around the sailings or take Main Street up to Five Corners. For a morning flight, plan an Edmonds pickup about 3.75 hours before departure on a weekday.

What Edmonds rides usually are

Edmonds books airport travel and a strong flow of cruise transfers — the waterfront demographic sails from the Seattle piers and prefers to be driven. We also do Kingston-ferry connections — dropping at the Edmonds terminal for foot passengers, or the full trip for people continuing to the Kitsap Peninsula. Swedish Edmonds and the medical offices book assisted transportation, and downtown Edmonds residents book point-to-point rides into Seattle for the theater and dinners.
