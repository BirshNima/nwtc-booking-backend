Federal Way to SEA is I‑5 north, with SR‑99 (Pacific Highway South) as the slower, stoplight-heavy backup. The northbound I‑5 stretch through Federal Way and Kent is a reliable morning slowdown — 15 minutes can become 40 between 7 and 9 — so we set the pickup with that in mind. From Twin Lakes and the west side, Dash Point Road and SW 320th get you to I‑5 or SR‑509. For a morning flight, plan a Federal Way pickup about 2.5 hours before departure on a weekday.

What Federal Way rides usually are

Federal Way is mostly airport travel — it's a bedroom community with an easy shot to SEA, so early departures and late arrivals are the bread and butter. St. Francis Hospital and the surrounding medical offices book assisted transportation. The redeveloped Weyerhaeuser campus and the offices near the Commons generate some corporate travel, and families book us for the trip to the cruise terminals and for group airport runs.
