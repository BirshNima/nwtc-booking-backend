For a morning flight from Redmond, plan the pickup about 3 hours before departure on a weekday; 2.5 is enough on a weekend.

What Redmond rides usually are

Redmond runs on corporate travel. The Microsoft campus, the game and hardware companies around it, and the biotech and cloud offices along Willows Road generate constant airport runs for employees, visiting executives, and interview candidates flown in for the day. These are exactly the trips a company wants on a single account: a flat rate, a car that's actually waiting, and one monthly invoice instead of a stack of rideshare receipts to reconcile.

Overlake — the hospital, the medical offices, the Kaiser campus — books calm, assisted airport and appointment transportation. Downtown Redmond and Education Hill residents book point-to-point rides into Seattle and Bellevue, and the occasional Woodinville tasting afternoon, which is 15 minutes north.

Redmond FAQ

Can you pick up from a specific Microsoft building?

Yes — the campus has dozens of buildings, so tell us the building number when you book and the driver will be at that lobby. For a visiting guest, we can coordinate with your front desk or EA.

Why is the Redmond rate higher than Bellevue's?

Redmond is a longer drive to Sea-Tac — roughly 35–45 minutes versus 25–35 from Bellevue. The flat rate reflects the distance, and it doesn't change with traffic.

Do you serve Overlake Medical Center?

Yes, for airport transfers and appointment transportation, with help in and out of the car and a driver who waits.

Can you run a Woodinville wine tour from Redmond?

Yes — it's a short hop north. Wine tours are booked hourly; see cruise & wine tours.
