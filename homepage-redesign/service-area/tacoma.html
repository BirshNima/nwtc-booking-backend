Tacoma to SEA is a straight shot up I‑5 north, and the whole trip lives or dies on that corridor. The stretch through Fife, Federal Way, and Kent is one of the region's most reliable slowdowns, and southbound I‑5 past the Tacoma Dome and the JBLM interchange backs up on weekday afternoons and event nights. Your driver checks the live conditions before pickup and, when I‑5 is a parking lot, uses SR‑167 or the surface arterials to get around the worst of it.

Because of that variability, plan a Tacoma airport pickup about 3 to 3.5 hours before a morning departure on a weekday, and give an afternoon flight the same margin — the drive is fine at 10 a.m. and brutal at 4 p.m.

What Tacoma rides usually are

Downtown Tacoma generates business and convention travel — the Greater Tacoma Convention Center, the Museum District hotels like Hotel Murano and the McMenamins Elks, and out-of-town speakers and clients who fly into SEA. Cruise transfers are a Tacoma booking too: many Pierce County residents sail from the Seattle piers and would rather be driven than park a car downtown for a week. We also serve the University of Puget Sound and Pacific Lutheran for family visits and move-in/move-out airport runs, and the areas around Joint Base Lewis‑McChord for service members and families.

Residential Tacoma — the North End, Proctor, Stadium, Point Ruston — books quiet, reliable airport service and point-to-point rides into Seattle for games, shows, and dinners.

Tacoma FAQ

Why does a Tacoma airport pickup need such a big buffer?

Because it's all I‑5, and the corridor through Fife, Federal Way, and Kent is unpredictable — 30 minutes off-peak, 70 in the worst of rush. We plan for the bad case so you're not sprinting through the terminal.

Can you take us to the Seattle cruise terminals from Tacoma?

Yes — Pier 66 (Bell Street) and Pier 91 (Smith Cove), timed to your cruise line's boarding window, with luggage help at the curb. See cruise & wine tours.

Do you serve JBLM and the DuPont / Lakewood area?

Yes, for airport transfers and point-to-point trips. Allow extra time if the pickup or drop-off is inside a base gate.
