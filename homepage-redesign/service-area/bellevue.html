Two routes carry most Bellevue airport traffic: I‑405 south to I‑5 through Tukwila, and I‑90 west to I‑5 south. Which one wins depends entirely on the hour — 405 through the Renton S‑curves is the usual bottleneck, and I‑90 across the lake backs up westbound in the afternoon. Your driver checks both before pickup and picks the one that's moving. From downtown Bellevue we also use Bellevue Way to 112th to get onto 405 cleanly rather than fighting the ramp at Main.

For a morning flight, a Bellevue pickup 2.5 hours before departure is right most days; move it to 3 hours for an early international flight or a Monday.
