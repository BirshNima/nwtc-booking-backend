What Auburn rides usually are

Auburn's economy runs on the valley warehouse and manufacturing district — Boeing's Auburn operations, the big distribution centers, and their suppliers — and that's where the corporate travel comes from: visiting staff, auditors, and crews who need reliable pickups and one monthly invoice.
