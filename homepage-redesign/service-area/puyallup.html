Puyallup to SEA runs SR‑512 west to I‑5 north, or SR‑167 (the Valley Freeway) up through the toll lanes to I‑405 and SR‑518. The SR‑512/I‑5 interchange is the reliable slow point, and SR‑167's toll lanes back up in the peaks. During the Washington State Fair in September, the streets around the fairgrounds and both freeway approaches are a different world — we route wide and leave extra time. For a morning flight, plan a Puyallup pickup about 3.5 hours before departure on a weekday.

What Puyallup rides usually are

Puyallup is largely airport travel for South Hill and downtown residents, plus medical transportation to and from Good Samaritan and the MultiCare campus. Pierce College Puyallup brings some student and family airport runs, and the Fair in September generates a burst of event trips. Residential Puyallup books quiet, reliable service and hourly rides for appointments and errands.

Puyallup FAQ

How long is the drive from Puyallup to Sea-Tac?

35–45 minutes off-peak via SR‑512 to I‑5, longer in weekday rush and much longer during the State Fair in September. The flat rate doesn't change with traffic.

Do you serve South Hill?

Yes — it's the same flat Puyallup rate to SEA, either direction. Extra stops are $20 each.

Can you get around the Fair traffic in September?

We route wide and leave extra time. Book earlier in the day when you can — the freeway approaches are the problem, not just the fairgrounds.

Do you do medical transportation to Good Samaritan?

Yes, for airport transfers and appointments, with help in and out of the car and a driver who waits.
