From most of Kirkland the route is I‑405 south to I‑90 west, then I‑5 south, or 405 all the way to SR‑518 through Tukwila. The I‑405 stretch past Bellevue and through the Renton S‑curves is the reliable slow point, so on weekday afternoons the driver leaves earlier and, if it's bad, cuts over on I‑90. From downtown Kirkland and Carillon Point we take Lake Washington Boulevard or Lake Street south to the 405 on-ramp at NE 68th rather than the congested downtown ramps. For a morning flight, a Kirkland pickup about 2.75 hours before departure is comfortable.

What Kirkland rides usually are

Kirkland's demand is a mix. Corporate — the Google Kirkland campus, the tech and design firms downtown and in the Carillon Point complex, and the medical employers around Totem Lake and EvergreenHealth — books airport runs and client travel on account. Weddings and events — the Woodmark Hotel and Carillon Point on the water, the Heathman downtown, and the marina venues host a steady wedding calendar, and those bookings need a driver who knows the exact entrance and can stage a couple of vehicles without blocking the lot.

Downtown Kirkland residents and the neighborhoods up on Rose Hill and in Juanita book point-to-point rides into Seattle for dinners and shows, and quiet, on-time airport service.
