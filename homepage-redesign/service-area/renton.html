Renton to SEA is short: I‑405 south to SR‑518 west, or Rainier Avenue and SR‑509 for the surface option when the freeway is jammed. The catch is that the I‑405 Renton S‑curves — the regional bottleneck everyone else on the Eastside complains about — start right here, so from the Highlands and Kennydale we often drop onto 405 further south to skip the worst of it. From downtown Renton and the Landing it's a straight shot. For a morning flight, a Renton pickup about 2 hours before departure is comfortable.

What Renton rides usually are

Renton's biggest travel generator is Boeing — the Renton plant that builds the 737, plus its suppliers and contractors, book a steady stream of airport runs and visiting-engineer pickups. Valley Medical Center and the surrounding clinics book assisted airport and appointment transportation. The Landing hotels and the offices around Southport on Lake Washington round it out, and Renton residents book point-to-point rides into Seattle and Bellevue and hourly service for a day of meetings.
