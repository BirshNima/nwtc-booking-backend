One-way or round trip

Book the return, or don't

Most point-to-point rides are one-way — you book the trip you need. If you want the same car to bring you back later, ask for a round trip when you enquire and we'll quote both legs together, holding the vehicle for you in between.

Wait time

A short stop is fine

A quick "wait while I run in" is included — 15 minutes on non-airport trips. Longer than that and it's $1.50 a minute, or you're better off booking hourly service, where the car stays with you all day.

Point-to-point FAQ

Quick answers

How is the price set?

SEA routes use our published flat rates. Everything else is quoted individually from the pickup, drop-off, and vehicle — you'll have the number before you commit, and it doesn't change.

How fast do I get a quote?

Usually within the hour during the day. For same-day rides, call — dispatch is staffed 24/7.

Can I add stops?

Yes. The first extra stop is included; additional stops are $20 each. Add them when you book so the quote and the timing are right.

Is gratuity included?

A 20% gratuity is shown as a separate line and included in the quoted price. You can adjust it.

How far ahead should I book?

A few hours is usually enough. Friday and Saturday evenings and event nights are busier — book earlier in the day.
