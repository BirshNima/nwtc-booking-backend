Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

How far ahead should I book?

For airport pickups, a day's notice is comfortable; we take same-day and last-minute rides whenever a car is free, so call if you're inside 24 hours. Weddings, events, and multi-vehicle bookings are best locked in two to three weeks out.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track it. Your pickup time moves with the flight automatically, and there's no charge for the wait on a delayed arrival.

How is pricing set?

Airport and hourly trips use published flat rates — you see the price before you book, and it doesn't change with traffic or demand. Weddings, events, and out-of-area trips are quoted individually, usually within the hour.

Do you charge for wait time?

Airport pickups include 60 minutes of wait; other pickups include 15. Beyond that it's $1.50 a minute. Parking and tolls are billed at actual cost.

What areas do you serve?

All of King and Pierce counties from a SeaTac base — Seattle, the Eastside, and the South Sound — plus North Sound cities like Everett and Edmonds. Ask about anywhere else.

Can you provide child seats?

Yes — infant, convertible, or booster, $15 each. Tell us the child's age and weight when you book.

How do I pay?

Individual trips are charged to a card after dispatch confirms the final quote. Corporate accounts are invoiced monthly.
