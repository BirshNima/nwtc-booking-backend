How billing works

Billed by the hour, from pickup

The clock starts at your scheduled pickup and ends at the final drop-off. Billed hours are whichever is greater: the time you use, or the minimum for that vehicle. If the day runs long, the extra time is added at the same hourly rate — no penalty, no surprise multiplier.

Leaving the area

Longer trips, same idea

Hourly works for a run to Chelan, Leavenworth, or the Olympic Peninsula too — you're still paying for time, and the driver stays with you. For long-distance days, call and we'll confirm the hours and any overnight arrangements up front.

Hourly FAQ

Before you book the day

What's the minimum?

Three hours for the sedan and SUV, four for the Sprinter van. If your plans only need a single one-way trip, book point-to-point instead — it'll be cheaper.

What if we run over the booked time?

Extra time is billed at the same hourly rate. Your driver will let you know as you approach the end of the booking; there's no overtime multiplier.

Can the driver leave and come back?

They can, for a long gap — a three-hour dinner, say — if you arrange it when booking. Otherwise the car and driver stay with you the whole time.

Is gratuity included?

A 20% gratuity is shown as a separate line and included in the price before you book. You can adjust it.

How far ahead should I book?

A day or two for weekday bookings; a week for Friday and Saturday evenings, which fill up.
