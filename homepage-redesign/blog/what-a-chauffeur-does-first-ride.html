Good to know

What a chauffeur actually does — and what to expect on your first ride

If you've only ever used a taxi or an app, a car service works a little differently. Here's the whole thing, plainly.

Northwest Town Car Service · updated September 2026 · 4 min read

A chauffeur is a professional driver assigned to your specific trip in advance. You book ahead, the price is set before you ride, the same vetted driver is confirmed the day before, and they handle the doors, the bags, the route, and the timing so you don't. It's less like hailing a ride and more like having a driver for the day.

How booking works

You reserve the trip — online or by phone — with the pickup, drop-off, date, and time. For airport and hourly trips you see the flat price before you confirm; other trips come back as a quick quote. Dispatch assigns a driver and vehicle, and the day before your trip you get a confirmation with the chauffeur's name and the car. There's no app to watch and no wondering whether a car will accept the ride.

What the chauffeur handles

Everything about the drive. They arrive early and are waiting when you come out. They open the door, take your bags, and load the trunk. They know the route and the alternatives, and they've already checked traffic before pickup. For an airport run they're tracking your flight. On an hourly booking they stay with you between stops. You get in, and the logistics stop being your problem.

What's in the car

A clean, well-kept vehicle — inspected and cleaned between trips — with bottled water and Wi-Fi. It's quiet enough to take a call or work. You choose how much conversation you want; a good chauffeur reads that and follows your lead.

Gratuity and payment

On our service a 20% gratuity is shown as a separate line and included in the price you see before booking, so there's nothing to calculate or hand over on the day — though you can adjust it. Individual trips are charged to a card after dispatch confirms the final quote; corporate accounts are invoiced monthly. You'll get an itemized receipt by email.

How it's different from a taxi or rideshare

Three things. It's scheduled — a specific driver committed to your trip, not the nearest available car. It's fixed-price on airport and hourly trips — no meter, no surge, the 4 a.m. holiday rate is the same as the Tuesday rate. And it's a licensed carrier — commercial insurance, permitted and vetted drivers, a real business you can call — which is why companies use it for executives and why it's the choice for a trip where being on time is the entire point.

Common questions

Do I need to tip the chauffeur?

A 20% gratuity is already shown as a separate line and included in the price you see before booking. You can adjust it, but there's nothing to hand over on the day.

How is a car service different from Uber?

It's scheduled to your specific trip with a named driver confirmed in advance, it's fixed-price on airport and hourly trips with no surge, and it's a licensed, insured carrier.

Will the same driver be there for my return trip?

For a round trip booked together, we hold the vehicle and, where possible, the same driver. Book both legs at once so the return is reserved.

What if I need to change the pickup time?

Call or text dispatch — (206) 596-5504, 24/7 — and we'll move it. That's the point of a scheduled service.
