Prom & homecoming transportation in the Seattle area, done right

A licensed, insured car and a vetted chauffeur, a sensible plan for the night, and the details parents actually want confirmed.

Northwest Town Car Service · updated September 2026 · 4 min read

For prom or homecoming, book a vehicle sized to the group — a sedan or SUV for a few couples, a Sprinter van for a larger party — with a vetted, licensed chauffeur and a fixed itinerary agreed in advance. Book three to four weeks out; the popular weekends in the spring and fall go early.

The right vehicle

Match the vehicle to the party. Two or three couples fit an executive sedan or a premium SUV comfortably, with room for dresses. A group of eight to fourteen wants a Sprinter van — everyone travels together, which is usually the point of the night. We steer families away from stretch limos and party buses for high-schoolers; a clean, professional SUV or van is the better call, and parents tend to agree.

The plan for the night

Agree the itinerary when you book: pickup points (often several homes), the dinner restaurant, the venue, and the return — one trip or a couple of drop-off loops. Build in buffer for the photo session that always runs long. The chauffeur follows the agreed plan; changes on the night go through the parent who booked, not the students.

The driver

Every trip is a licensed, permitted, background-checked chauffeur in a commercially insured vehicle — the same standard as our executive work. On a prom booking the driver's job is straightforward: get the group safely between the agreed stops, on time, and keep the parent who booked informed. There's no bar, no light show, and no ambiguity about who's responsible.

What parents should confirm

  • The flat price and what it covers (our prom bookings are hourly, with the gratuity included in the quote).
  • The itinerary in writing — every stop and the return.
  • Who the driver contacts if plans change (you, not the students).
  • The vehicle and capacity, so the group actually fits.
  • That the company is a licensed WUTC carrier with a certificate of insurance available.

Common questions

How far ahead should we book prom transportation in Seattle?

Three to four weeks. The popular spring and fall weekends fill early, especially for Sprinter vans that fit a whole group.

Do you do stretch limos or party buses for prom?

We recommend against them for high-schoolers and offer clean, professional SUVs and Sprinter vans instead — safer, and what most parents prefer.

Who does the driver call if plans change on the night?

The parent who made the booking, not the students. The itinerary is agreed in advance and changes go through you.

Is gratuity included in a prom booking?

Yes — prom bookings are hourly with the gratuity included in the quoted price.
