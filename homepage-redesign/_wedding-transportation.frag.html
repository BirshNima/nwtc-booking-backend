Wedding FAQ

What couples and planners ask

How far ahead should we book?

Two to three months for a full fleet on a peak Saturday. A single couple's car can often be arranged with a few weeks' notice, but summer weekends go early.

What deposit do you take?

A deposit against the quoted total holds the vehicles. The balance is due before the event. Exact terms are on your written quote.

Can the car be decorated?

Yes — magnetic signs, ribbon, and a "just married" placard are fine. We'll agree anything adhesive in advance.

What if the reception runs long?

The plan is built with buffer. If the day genuinely overruns, extra time is added at the vehicle's hourly rate and your coordinator confirms it with you, not a surprise on the invoice.

Can you handle multiple venues?

That's the common case — a hotel, a ceremony site, a reception venue. The written timeline maps every leg and staging point.

Do you cover venues outside Seattle?

Across King and Pierce counties as standard, and wine-country and island venues by arrangement. Tell us the location when you enquire.
