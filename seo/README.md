# SEO growth plan — Northwest Town Car Service

Step 9 of the revenue core. Local-first, commercial-intent, measured to bookings.

**Live:** https://claude.ai/code/artifact/7b952d8c-d048-4b92-bf9d-20acedd2b6af

## What's in `index.html`

1. **Strategy** — commercial intent over traffic, local before national, measured to revenue; what we won't do (doorway pages, stuffing, fake signals, bought links).
2. **Keyword clusters** — 6 clusters (airport, corporate, general, wedding, hourly, cruise/wine) + per-city location terms, each mapped to one anchor page with intent.
3. **URL & intent map** — the full site: 6 live service pages (optimize), ~10–14 city pages (build), 12 blog posts, supporting pages.
4. **Page briefs** — full briefs (URL / primary + secondary keywords / intent / title / meta / H1 / H2s / internal links / CTA / schema / content requirements) for the homepage, all 5 core service pages, a city-page template (Bellevue), and the 4 flagship blog posts.
5. **Location-page discipline** — build order, the "find-and-replace test", and the required elements for every city page.
6. **Internal-link model** — hub-and-spoke rules.
7. **Content calendar** — 12 posts over 12 months, front-loaded by cluster value.
8. **Local SEO & GBP** — profile setup, review velocity (2–4/week via the CRM automation), NAP consistency, weekly posts.
9. **Citations, links & digital PR** — core citations, local partnerships (hotels, venues, chambers), opportunistic PR; what to avoid.
10. **Technical checklist** — GSC, sitemap, canonicals, schema, Core Web Vitals, 301s from the old site, thin-page noindex.
11. **Measurement** — the `impression → click → lead → quote → booking → fare` funnel, GA4 events (the funnel already emits them), CallRail, and the CRM's `source` stamp from `intake.js`. A monthly scorecard.
12. **90-day & 12-month plan** — days 1–30 (foundation), 31–60 (money pages), 61–90 (expand), then quarterly.

## Depends on
