02 Quote follow-up sequence

comms/sequences.js is a pure state machine. An hourly job runs nextAction(lead) over every open quote and sends whatever it returns. The clock starts at Quote Sent At.

+168 h

Stops immediately on any of: Lead Status becomes Booked / Won / Lost / Cancelled  ·  the customer sends any inbound reply (Customer Replied)  ·  they text STOP (optedOut)  ·  the pickup date passes. A manual-quote Lead with no Quote Sent At returns handoff — dispatch prices it before the sequence can start.

03 Channel policy

Launch runbook, decision 5.

  • Transactional — acknowledgement, deposit link, deposit paid, 24 h reminder, receipt: sms email both. These are expected and time-sensitive.
  • Quote follow-up — day 0 and day 1 by sms (fast, high open rate while intent is warm); day 3 and day 7 by email (room for the full recap, lower pressure).
  • Marketing / win-back — not here. That is a separate list with its own express opt-in, driven from the CRM "Marketing Opt-in" field.

04 SMS compliance

  • A2P 10DLC — register the brand and a campaign with Twilio before the first send. Carrier approval is 1–3 weeks; unregistered traffic is filtered. Use a Messaging Service (TWILIO_MESSAGING_SERVICE_SID) so the campaign and number pool are handled for you.
  • Consent — the funnel's checkbox ("text and email me about this reservation") writes Consent on the Lead and Marketing Opt-in on the Customer. plan() will not produce an SMS when smsConsent === false.
  • STOP / START / HELP — send.js handleInboundSms() handles them: STOP sets SMS Opt-out and confirms; START clears it; HELP replies with the business name and number. Point the Twilio number's inbound webhook at that handler.
  • Quiet hours — quietHoursShift() pushes any SMS scheduled between 20:00 and 08:00 PT to 08:05. Email is unaffected.
  • Every marketing-adjacent SMS carries "Reply STOP to opt out." Transactional confirmations don't need it but the deposit and review texts include it anyway.
  • Email — SPF + DKIM on the domain before the first send; a List-Unsubscribe header on anything non-transactional.

05 Failure handling

  • Dedupe — every send is keyed <Reference>:<templateId> and checked against the Messages table first. A retried webhook or a double cron tick can't double-send.
  • Retry — one retry after 1.5 s on a 5xx from Twilio or the email API. A 4xx (bad number, unsubscribed) fails fast.
  • Logging — each attempt writes a Messages row (Sent / Failed + provider id + error) and an Activity Log line on the Lead.
  • Escalation — any Failed send posts to DISPATCH_WEBHOOK_URL so a person can call the customer. The 24 h reminder failing is treated as urgent.
  • Dry run — COMMS_DRY_RUN=1 renders and logs every message without sending, for staging.

06 Deploy & test

  1. Buy the Twilio number; start 10DLC brand + campaign registration the same day.
  2. Stand up the email sender (Postmark / SES / Resend), add SPF + DKIM records, verify book@northwesttowncarservice.com.
  3. Add the Messages table to the Airtable base; add Customer Replied, Needs Reply, Followup Steps Sent to Leads and SMS Opt-out to Customers.
  4. .env from config.example.env. Set COMMS_DRY_RUN=1 for now.
  5. Wire the CRM automations (step 3 doc) to call deliverAll(NWTCComms.plan(trigger, ctx)) on each event.
  6. Schedule the hourly follow-up job: for each open quote Lead, NWTCSequence.nextAction() → if send, deliver and append the step.
  7. Point the Twilio inbound-SMS webhook at handleInboundSms. Text STOP from your phone; confirm the opt-out row and the sequence halting.
  8. Dry-run one of each: reservation, instant quote, deposit link, reminder (with a fake driver), review at rating 5 and rating 2 (only 5 should send).
  9. Flip COMMS_DRY_RUN=0. Send yourself a live confirmation and a live follow-up. Check both render correctly on a phone.
