# Corporate outreach templates — Northwest Town Car Service

Use with the CRM corporate pipeline (Prospect → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal
→ Negotiation → Account Created). Stop the sequence the moment someone replies or
books. Everyone who doesn't convert goes to a quarterly re-touch.

Placeholders: `{FirstName}` `{Company}` `{City}` `{TheirTitle}` `{YourName}`

---

## 1. LinkedIn connection request (≤ 300 chars)

> Hi {FirstName} — I run Northwest Town Car Service, a Seattle-area chauffeur

## 7. Pilot proposal — one-pager outline

Send after a qualifying call. Keep it to one page.

1. **Header** — {Company} × Northwest Town Car Service · prepared {date}
2. **What you told us** — 2–3 bullets on their travel (volume, types, who books)
3. **Your account** —
   - Discount tier: **Preferred (10% off)** for the pilot month, then reassessed against volume
   - Dedicated dispatch line: {number}
   - Authorized bookers: {names}
   - Billing: monthly consolidated invoice, Net 15
4. **Rates** — the standard flat-rate table with the 10% applied, plus hourly rates
5. **Included** — flight tracking, meet & greet, 60-min airport wait, itemized receipts, COI on file
6. **Getting started** — sign the one-page account agreement → bookers get the line → first ride
7. **Contact** — {YourName}, {email}, (206) 596-5504
